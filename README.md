## E-commerce Sales Analysis using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyses e-commerce sales data using SQL to answer business questions and identify sales trends and employee performance.

The goal of this project was to practise SQL analysis and develop the ability to turn sales data into meaningful business insights.
## Business Questions

This project uses SQL to answer business questions related to sales performance, customers, products, orders, and revenue.

The analysis covers:

- Total revenue, orders, customers, and products
- Average order value
- Top-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Top customers by revenue
- Monthly revenue trends
- Revenue by city
- Repeat customers
- Customers who have never placed an order
- Inactive customers
- Best-selling product category
- Highest and lowest revenue-generating products
- Average products per order
- High-value orders
- Customers with the highest average order value

## Key Findings

The analysis produced several insights into the company's sales and customer performance:

* **Electronics generated the highest revenue** among the product categories analysed, making it the strongest-performing category in the dataset.
* **The Laptop generated the highest total revenue** among individual products.
* **The Keyboard generated the lowest total revenue**, indicating an opportunity to review its pricing, demand, or marketing performance.
* **Rahul generated the highest customer revenue** based on the available purchase data.
* **Amit was identified as a repeat customer**, having placed more than one order.
* **Eohan had not placed an order**, representing a potential customer-conversion opportunity.
* The available order data covers **March 2025**, meaning the current dataset does not contain enough months to identify longer-term seasonal trends.
* Customers and cities generating higher revenue represent important segments for retention, targeted marketing, and future growth strategies.

## Business Insights & Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several business actions could be considered:

* Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products and categories, particularly Electronics and the Laptop.
* Use cross-selling and upselling strategies around high-revenue products.
* Develop loyalty or personalised marketing campaigns for repeat and high-value customers.
* Target customers who have never purchased with first-order discounts or welcome campaigns.
* Investigate lower-performing products to determine whether pricing, marketing, product demand, or inventory decisions are affecting performance.
* Use additional monthly sales data to monitor trends, measure campaign performance, and support future sales forecasting.
